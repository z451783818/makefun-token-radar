How to read a farm plan

  
Each row carries five steps built from its own figures: a setup step, the core action for its category, a repeat cadence, a watch step for points or quest programs, and a monthly re-check against the radar's figures. Costs are starting points for a first position, not advice; a protocol that records no token may never issue one, and farming costs gas and time whether or not a launch follows.
